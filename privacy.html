Privacy Policy

The shortest privacy policy you will read today.

TL;DR: We do not collect your data. We cannot see your data. Your files, text and results are processed entirely in your browser and never leave your device.

What happens when you use a tool

Every calculation — from percentage math to image resizing — runs as JavaScript on your own machine. When you resize an image or extract a palette, the file is read by your browser locally and drawn to a canvas element. No upload ever occurs.

What we do not have

  • No accounts, no logins, no sign-up forms — so we hold no names, emails or passwords.
  • No analytics scripts running on your visits.
  • No databases storing your inputs or results — there is no backend to store them in.
  • No cookies set by us.

Third-party requests

The site loads fonts and animation libraries from public CDNs (Google Fonts, cdnJs, jsDelivr) so pages render and animate nicely. Those providers may see standard request logs, like any website request. If you prefer zero external requests, an offline build is available on request.

Your control

Because everything is local, closing the tab erases every trace of your work. Clearing your browser storage removes anything the site may have cached. There is nothing more to delete — and nothing more to leak.
