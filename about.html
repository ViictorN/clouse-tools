About Clouse Tools

Simple tools. Real productivity. Free, forever, for everyone.

Clouse Tools is a library of 67 free online tools built around one simple idea: the fastest path from a question to an answer. No sign-up walls, no uploads, no "premium" upsells — you open a tool, you type, you get a result.

Our promise: every tool runs 100% inside your browser. Your text, dates, passwords and images are processed locally on your own device and never sent to any server — because there is no server to send them to.

Why we built it

Most "free online tools" are covered in ads, demand accounts and quietly upload your files. We kept bumping into that and decided to build the opposite: a clean, fast, private toolbox with the everyday utilities people actually use — percentages, dates, text, units, images and the small developer helpers you always need and can never find.

How it works

  • Instant by default — results update live as you type, with no "Calculate" round-trips.
  • Keyboard-first — press Ctrl + K (or /) anywhere to search all 67 tools.
  • Private by design — images are resized, compressed and analyzed on canvas, in your browser; hashes and passwords use your device's secure random source.
  • Accessible — semantic HTML, focus states, reduced-motion support and AA contrast throughout.

What is inside

Six categories cover the everyday needs: Math (percentage, averages, loans, BMI), Text (counting, cases, sorting, diff), Date & Time (differences, ages, countdowns, time zones), Unit Converters (length, weight, temperature, data and more), Developer (Base64, JSON, regex, SHA-256, JWT) and Design (image resizing, gradients, QR codes, palettes).
